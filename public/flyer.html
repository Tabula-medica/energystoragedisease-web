Type 2 Diabetes Is Misnamed. Here's the Root Cause.

"Diabetes mellitus" describes glucosuria — an overflow symptom. Energy Storage Disease (ESD) names the underlying mechanism: a cascade of insulin resistance, metabolic inflexibility, chronic energy surplus, and mitochondrial dysfunction. Reframing changes how patients understand — and how clinicians treat.

Cellular Energy Lock (Insulin Resistance) — Cells resist insulin signaling; glucose builds up as overflow, not as cause

Standard Panel (Misses ESD)

  • Fasting glucose — lags by years
  • HbA1c — reflects overflow, not cause
  • Total cholesterol — poor ESD marker
  • No HOMA-IR ordered
  • No fasting insulin ordered

ESD-Aware Panel (Detects Early)

  • Fasting insulin (optimal <5 μIU/mL)
  • HOMA-IR (optimal <1.0)
  • TG/HDL ratio (optimal <1.5)
  • Uric acid (<5.5 mg/dL)
  • HbA1c in context of above

Clinical Evidence for Reversal

46% remission at 12 months through intensive dietary intervention alone — DiRECT trial (Lean et al., Lancet 2018)
60% reversal at 2 years with continuous ketogenic nutritional intervention vs 6% usual care (Hallberg et al., Front. Endocrinol. 2019)
Liver and pancreatic fat clear within 8 weeks of sustained energy deficit, restoring beta-cell function (Taylor, Twin Cycle Hypothesis 2019)
Metabolic inflexibility is measurable and reversible with substrate cycling and exercise-induced mitochondrial biogenesis (Kelley & Mandarino 2000; Mootha 2003)
